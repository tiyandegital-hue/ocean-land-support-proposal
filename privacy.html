Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This page explains, in plain language, what data this website collects and why. It is written for the law of Monaco (Law No. 1.565 on the protection of personal data) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect

Enquiry form. When you send an enquiry, we collect your name, your email address, what needs managing, the disciplines you pick, and your timeline if you give one. We collect only what is needed to reply to your enquiry.

Contact by email or phone. If you write to info@oceanlandsupport.com or call +33 643 911 447, we receive whatever you choose to send us.

Cookies. See the Cookie Policy. Analytics cookies are used only if you accept them.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to agree a scope of work with you, and to provide the services you ask for. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have separately agreed to that.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any work that follows from it, and then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to info@oceanlandsupport.com and we will respond. You also have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority in Monaco or in your own country.

Who to contact
