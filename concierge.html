What concierge covers

Itineraries, activities, travel, on-board life.

  • Itineraries — days planned around how you actually like to spend them.
  • Activities & excursions — ashore and afloat, arranged in advance.
  • Travel — the journeys around the plan, coordinated for you.
  • On-board life — provisioning and the details that make time on board effortless.

Exclusive to our clientele

A private concierge, reserved for our clientele.
