Questions

Asked, answered honestly.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your vessel or home, the scope of what you ask us to take on, and the length of the agreement. We do not publish set prices. Tell us what needs managing and we will discuss cost with you directly — nothing is agreed until you have it in writing.

What exactly do you manage?

Yachts: technical, operational, financial, crew, safety and compliance, insurance, provisioning, berthing and fuelling, and itinerary planning. Homes: day-to-day running, maintenance, housekeeping, security and arrival preparation. And concierge for our clientele and their charters.

Who will I deal with?

Vanessa Matten and Sarah Leclézio, the founders. You have one point of contact — no relay between departments.

Do you work outside Monaco?

Yes. The firm is based in Monaco and supports clients worldwide, 24/7.
