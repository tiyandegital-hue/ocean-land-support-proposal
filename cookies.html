What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. This site uses only the cookies listed below.

CookiePurposeWhen it is set
ols_consentRemembers your cookie choice (accept analytics, or essential only).When you answer the cookie banner.
ols_secure_sessionKeeps your session secure while you browse.Essential — always set.
ols_wizard_stateRemembers where you are in the enquiry wizard if you leave and come back.Essential — only while you use the wizard.
ols_ga4_anonymousAnonymous analytics — how the site is used, without identifying you.Only if you accept analytics in the banner.
ols_whatsapp_intentRemembers which service you selected before choosing WhatsApp.Only when you use the WhatsApp entry point.

Your choice

Use the cookie banner to choose “Accept analytics” or “Essential only”. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser’s cookies for this site — the banner will appear again on your next visit.
